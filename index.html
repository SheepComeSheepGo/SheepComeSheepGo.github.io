Privacy Policy

Sheep Come Sheep Go · Last updated June 2026

Short version: Sheep Come Sheep Go does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game runs entirely on your device and works fully offline. There are no accounts, no ads, and no third-party tracking.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or any identifier. The app contains no analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or social media trackers.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your game progress — coins, stars, unlocked levels, daily check-in streak, owned cosmetics, and settings — is saved locally on your device using the system preferences store. This data never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app or use "Reset all progress" in Settings.

3. Network Use

The game does not require an internet connection and does not transmit data to any server. No information is sent off the device during normal play.

4. Children's Privacy

The game is suitable for all ages (rated 4+). Because we collect no personal data, the app complies with children's privacy regulations such as COPPA and GDPR-K. There is no behavioral advertising and no data collection from anyone, including children.

5. Permissions

The app may use haptic feedback. It does not request access to the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts, or notifications.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in a future app update with a revised date above.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through the app's App Store support page or by email at kalpanaapte@icloud.com.
